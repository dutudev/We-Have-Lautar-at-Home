# We Have Lautar at Home
![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Entertainment_%26_Culture-Winner-gold)
## A Folk Rhythm Game

Step into the shoes of a well-known lăutar who was met with a terrible fate: his beloved saxophone snaps in half. He has no choice but to play his old, forgotten drums. Prove your button-pressing skills and bring Romanian folk to modern audiences—his career depends on it.

## Features

- Osu-like rhythm style game  
- Great art  
- 6 Romanian folkloric songs  
- Tactile input system (if you use drums)
